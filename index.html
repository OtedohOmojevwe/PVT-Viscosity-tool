How it works

The tool uses straight-line interpolation between the two nearest measured viscosity points. If a DL pressure lies outside the measured range, it uses the nearest two points to perform linear extrapolation.

μ(P) = μ₁ + [(P − P₁)(μ₂ − μ₁) / (P₂ − P₁)]

This is a data-matching utility, not a substitute for measured laboratory data or a full PVT model.

Privacy

Your data stays in your browser. This standalone tool does not require an account, database, backend or file upload. Calculations are performed locally on your device.
